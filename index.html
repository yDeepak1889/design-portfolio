About Me

I design at the intersection of logic and beauty

I've spent the last seven years working with remote product teams, VC-backed startups, and luxury design agencies. I build digital experiences the way a tailor builds a garment: with utility, precision, and a quiet obsession with how things feel. My practice is simple: grids, hierarchy, and the quiet confidence that comes from a system that works.

To me, great design doesn't shout—it shows up in the small things: the way a page breathes, the rhythm of a layout, and the systems that keep things feeling intentional even as things grow. I'm most proud of work that feels effortless, not loud.
